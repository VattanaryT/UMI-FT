# Cap commanded orientation at 3° per step

**What changed:** The r4 runtime remembers the last commanded `R_d` and slews the next chunk from it, at most 3° per step across the seam and inside the chunk. A step that hits the cap logs `rd_slew`.

**Why:** On `gt_replay_ep0_1794952662` the command reversed 8–32° once per replan from h18, while the demo tool turned smoothly (steps ≤ 2°, seams ≤ 1.8°). `ΔR = R_fieldᵀ R_tool`, so `R_field @ ΔR` is the demo tool when the decode frame matches the label frame. A walked frame leaves the cancelling `ΔR` as a world-frame twist. The foot cap does not limit `ΔR`.

**Result / status:** Offline ep0 with the slew, the 10 mm foot cap, and the face tracker: max per-step rotation 3.0°, max foot step 8.7 mm. Over h15–h20 the commanded orientation stays within 0.6° of the demo until h17, then 3–10° at h18, 10–24° at h19, and 18° down to 6° at h20. Not run on the robot.
